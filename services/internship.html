Overview

Learn with a team that hires for real

The SNR NEST internship program gives students and freshers a real look at how a growing company works. We offer eight structured internship roles across hiring, manpower, operations and digital work.

Every role comes with a fixed duration, a named mentor and clear learning goals. Interns work on real tasks, attend regular reviews and receive a certificate when they complete the program.
