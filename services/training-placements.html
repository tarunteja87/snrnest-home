Overview

Two supports, one goal

Many candidates have the ability but lose out at the interview stage because no one showed them how to prepare. SNR NEST provides practical training and Placements that build the skills companies look for.

Sessions are simple and hands-on: communication practice, resume guidance and mock interviews with honest feedback. We train candidates for BPO, operations and office roles and support them until they are ready to face an interview.
